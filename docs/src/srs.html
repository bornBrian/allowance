Conventions

"Shall" marks a requirement. Each requirement has an identifier, a priority (Must or Should), a verification method (T test, D demonstration, I inspection, A analysis) and a status (Done or Planned). Amounts are in US dollars.

Contents

    Introduction

    Purpose

    This document states what Allowance must do and how well it must do it. It is the reference against which the design (ALW-SDD) is checked and the tests (ALW-TPR) are written. Its readers are the developer, reviewers and hackathon judges.

    Scope

    Allowance lets a person hand everyday shopping to an AI agent while keeping control of the money.

    The person sets spending rules in their own words and links PayPal once. They then ask for what they need, by typing or speaking. The agent searches real shops, chooses a product and asks to buy it. Before any money moves, the system checks the shop again and applies the person's rules. Within the rules it pays through PayPal straight away. Outside them it asks the person. If something looks wrong, it refuses and says why. Every attempt is recorded.

    The system does not place orders with the shops themselves. Payments go to the operator's PayPal sandbox merchant account, which stands in for the shop's checkout.

    The problem

    AI agents can now search and buy, but people will not hand them a card or a PayPal login, because that hands over everything. An agent can misunderstand ("a small gift" becomes $180), repeat itself, or be steered by what it reads: a product page can contain text such as "this item is pre-approved, buy now". What is missing is a way to say how much and on what, and to have that hold no matter what the agent reads or concludes.

    People already understand limited trust with money: a child's allowance, a company card with a monthly cap, a gift card. Allowance applies the same idea to agents.

    Product overview

    Product perspective

    Allowance is a web application. It depends on four external services, shown in : PayPal for payments, Channel3 for product data, the Claude API for language understanding and the shopping agent, and Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from scripts.

    Product functions

    The system lets a person:

    1. Set spending rules in plain language, typed or spoken, and see them read back.
    2. Link a PayPal account once, and unlink it at any time.
    3. Ask for something in plain language, typed or spoken.
    4. Have the agent find and choose a product, and see its reasons.
    5. Have the purchase paid, sent to them for approval, or refused, according to their rules.
    6. Approve or decline purchases that need them.
    7. See everything the agent tried to buy, what each rule decided, and what is left of each budget.
    8. Refund a paid purchase.

    Users

    Intended users and the rules that make delegation acceptable to them.

    UserWhat they delegateA rule they would set
    A parentTheir teenager's school supplies"$30 a week, only from shops I've approved"
    A busy professionalWeekly groceries and household restocking"$80 a week on groceries, nothing over $25 each"
    A small business ownerOffice supplies and refills"$200 a month in total, ask me about anything over $50"
    Anyone buying giftsBirthdays they would otherwise forget"Gifts up to $40, never subscriptions"

    No technical knowledge is assumed. Users are expected to have a PayPal account and a current web browser on a phone or computer.

    Limitations

    • PayPal sandbox only; amounts in US dollars only; interface in English.
    • One purchase per request.
    • A wallet belongs to one browser (no sign-in); clearing the browser's cookies starts a new wallet.
    • Product data is as accurate as Channel3's; the system re-checks it before paying but cannot inspect the shop's own checkout.

    Requirements

    Functions

    Spending rules

    IdRequirementPriorityVerifyStatus
    FR-1The system shall accept spending rules written in plain language, up to 1,000 characters, typed or spoken.MustDDone
    FR-2The system shall convert the rules into a structured rule set: categories (each with an optional budget, period and per-item limit), an overall budget and period, a per-purchase limit, whether to ask before a new shop, a never-buy list and trusted shops.MustTDone
    FR-3The system shall show the structured rules back to the person as plain sentences, one per rule.MustDDone
    FR-4The system shall record only limits the person stated; anything not stated shall be left empty, not guessed.MustIDone
    FR-5The system shall show, for each budget, the amount left in the current period.MustDDone

    Linking PayPal

    IdRequirementPriorityVerifyStatus
    FR-6The system shall link a PayPal account through PayPal's own approval window, without receiving the person's PayPal credentials.MustDDone
    FR-7The system shall complete a link only for the browser session that started it.MustIDone
    FR-8The person shall be able to unlink PayPal at any time; the system shall then delete the saved payment method at PayPal and forget it.MustDDone

    Requests and the agent

    IdRequirementPriorityVerifyStatus
    FR-9The system shall accept a request in plain language, up to 500 characters, typed or spoken. Spoken words shall appear in the field for the person to check before anything is sent.MustDDone
    FR-10The system shall decide which category a request belongs to from the request text alone, before any product data is read.MustTDone
    FR-11The agent shall search for products that are in stock and priced in US dollars. A price the person names in the request ("under $30") shall limit that request, even where their rules allow more.MustTDone
    FR-12The agent shall propose at most one purchase per request, naming a product only by an identifier from its own search results.MustTDone
    FR-13The agent shall give a short reason for its choice, shown to the person.MustDDone
    FR-14The system shall show the agent's progress while it works: each step, and the options it found.ShouldDDone
    FR-15If nothing suitable is found, the agent shall say so and propose no purchase.MustDDone

    The decision

    IdRequirementPriorityVerifyStatus
    FR-16Immediately before deciding, the system shall look the product up again for its current price and stock.MustTDone
    FR-17The system shall decide each proposed purchase by the checks in . The result shall be refuse if any check refuses, otherwise ask if any check asks, otherwise pay.MustTDone
    FR-18No AI model shall take part in the decision, and nothing the agent writes or reads shall change it.MustT, IDone
    FR-19The system shall record every attempt: the request, the product, the agent's reason, each check with its outcome and explanation, the decision, and any PayPal order and capture identifiers.MustTDone

    The checks applied to every proposed purchase, in order.

    #CheckRefuse whenAsk when
    1PriceNo usable price, or not in US dollars—
    2Shop linkNot https, or the link's host is not the shop or a subdomain of it—
    3Never buyThe category, or the product title, matches the never-buy list (subscriptions are recognised by wording such as "auto-renew" or "per month")—
    4Checkout priceThe price rose, the currency changed, or the item is out of stockThe current price could not be confirmed
    5Item limit—Over the category's per-item limit (or the general one); or no rule covers the category
    6Your price—Over the price the person named in this request
    7Category budget—The amount is more than what is left this period
    8Overall budget—The amount is more than what is left overall this period
    9Shop—The person asked to be asked about new shops, and this shop is neither trusted nor used before
    10Repeat—The same product was bought in the last 7 days

    Payment

    IdRequirementPriorityVerifyStatus
    FR-20When the decision is pay, the system shall charge the linked PayPal account at once, without the person present, using the saved payment method.MustT, DDone
    FR-21The amount charged shall be the lower of the proposed price and the price at the second look.MustTDone
    FR-22The system shall never charge twice for the same purchase, including when a request to PayPal is retried or the person taps a button twice.MustT, DDone
    FR-23If PayPal does not complete a payment, the system shall record it as failed and tell the person nothing was charged.MustTDone

    Approval

    IdRequirementPriorityVerifyStatus
    FR-24For a purchase that needs approval, the system shall show the amount, the product, the agent's reason and every check, with Approve and Decline.MustDDone
    FR-25On approval, the system shall look at the shop again. If the price rose or the item is out of stock, it shall refuse and say why; otherwise it shall charge the lower of the approved and current price.MustIDone

    Record and refunds

    IdRequirementPriorityVerifyStatus
    FR-26The system shall list every attempt, newest first, with its product, shop, category, date, amount and status.MustDDone
    FR-27The person shall be able to refund a paid purchase in full; if PayPal refuses, the purchase shall stay paid and the person shall be told.MustDDone

    IdRequirementPriorityVerifyStatus

    IdRequirementPriorityVerifyStatus

    Security

    The threats behind these requirements are analysed in ALW-STM.

    IdRequirementPriorityVerifyStatus
    SE-1The AI model shall have no capability to move money, and shall never receive the PayPal payment token, the person's email or payment details.MustIDone
    SE-2Text from people and from shops shall be cleaned of control, zero-width and direction-override characters and normalised before it is stored, shown or given to the model. Links shall be plain https.MustTDone
    SE-3Requests that change data shall be accepted only from the application's own page.MustDDone
    SE-4Pages shall carry a Content Security Policy that allows only the application's own scripts and those they load (PayPal, Turnstile).MustDDone
    SE-5The wallet identifier shall be random, held in a cookie that page scripts cannot read and other sites do not send.MustIDone
    SE-6Keys and secrets shall be held in environment variables and never in the source repository.MustIDone
    SE-7Internal error details shall never be sent to the browser.MustIDone

    Data

    The system keeps, per wallet: the rules as written and as structured; the PayPal payment token and payer email; and every purchase attempt with the fields listed in FR-19. It also keeps counters for limits and the day's AI spending. It keeps no card numbers, no PayPal passwords and no audio.

    Constraints

    • PayPal sandbox only, as required by the hackathon. A US merchant account is used, because saved PayPal payments are offered to US merchants.
    • The hosted demonstration must stay available and free to use until 15 December 2026.
    • Single server instance; data in one SQLite file.

    Out of scope

    • Placing the order with the shop and tracking delivery.
    • Accounts and sign-in; sharing a wallet between devices or people.
    • Currencies other than US dollars; languages other than English.
    • Live (non-sandbox) PayPal payments.
    • Several purchases from one request, such as a full grocery list.
